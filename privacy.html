LeBread Ops

Privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Who is responsible

LeBread Ops is run by the leBread team admin. Contact: barista.lebread@gmail.com.

What data we store

  • Name, phone number, email address and Telegram user ID
  • Role, skill level, availability and planned shifts
  • Extra or missing minutes, days off and shift swaps
  • Sick-leave days (only the date and hours, never a diagnosis)
  • Hourly rate. This is used only to calculate sick-leave pay at 80%.
  • The messages you send to the assistant

Why

We use this data only to plan shifts, record working time and prepare the monthly hours summary. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell or share it for any other purpose.

Who sees it

  • You see your own hours and shifts.
  • The admin sees all team data to run the system.
  • The employer receives each person's monthly hours summary.
  • The staff group sees only shift changes, for example "Tuesday is covered by Ana". It never sees hours, pay or sick leave.

Where it is processed

  • The main database runs on a computer in Switzerland operated by the admin.
  • Telegram delivers the chat messages.
  • Anthropic (the Claude AI model) processes the text of your messages to understand requests.
  • Google (Calendar, Drive, Sheets, Gmail on the account barista.lebread@gmail.com) stores the calendar, the reports and the backups.

Some of these providers may process data outside Switzerland.

Google user data

LeBread Ops accesses only the leBread Google account. It uses Calendar to manage the shift calendar, Drive and Sheets to store the reports it creates, and Gmail to send monthly summaries to the employer. It does not read existing email. Its use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long we keep it

Working-time records are kept as long as Swiss labour law requires. Other data is deleted when you leave the team, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time. Write in the chat or email barista.lebread@gmail.com.
