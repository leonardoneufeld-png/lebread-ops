LeBread Ops

An internal assistant for the leBread team. It replaces our paper sheet for extra minutes worked.

What it does

  • Team members log extra or missing minutes, day-off requests and sick days in a Telegram chat.
  • It keeps each person's worked hours for the current pay period.
  • It puts shifts and changes on a shared Google Calendar.
  • Every Monday it sends each person their own hours so far.
  • Each month it creates an hours summary per person. That summary goes to the person and to the employer.

Who can use it

Only leBread staff added by the admin. It is not a public service and not open for sign-up.
